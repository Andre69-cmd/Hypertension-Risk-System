🎯 Purpose

A comprehensive Hypertension Risk Early Warning System that combines machine learning, explainable AI, and complete care coordination to empower patients and clinicians.

🛠 Technology Stack

  • Backend: Flask 2.3, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login
  • ML: Scikit-learn (Logistic Regression)
  • Explainability: SHAP
  • Reports: ReportLab (PDF), built-in CSV
  • Maps: Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap
  • Telemedicine: Jitsi Meet
  • QR: qrcode[pil]
  • i18n: Custom translation layer (EN/FR/SW)
  • Deployment: Gunicorn + WSGI
